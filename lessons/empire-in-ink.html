Explain the chain in your own words

Use at least three links from the chain. Show how one event led to the next.

Japan's rapid modernisation after the Meiji Restoration gave it the military strength to build an empire, but the unequal Washington Naval Treaty and the Great Depression left ordinary Japanese people angry and hungry. Militarist leaders used this anger to seize control of the government by 1936. Needing raw materials, they invaded Manchuria, and when the League of Nations failed to stop them, Japan felt free to invade China in 1937. As the West grew distracted by war in Europe, Japan expanded further into Southeast Asia, provoking a US oil embargo that made war seem like the only way to survive. This chain of cause and effect ended with the attack on Pearl Harbor in 1941.
